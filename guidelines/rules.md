# Engine rules

Rules that apply to every project, next to the project's own guideline rules. Generated by [`build_rules.py`](./build_rules.py); do not edit by hand. The writing of the MACH-, MSV-, OWT-, OWA-, REUSE-, NFR- and SOA- rules is the factory's own, framed so it can be checked on a specification or a build.
